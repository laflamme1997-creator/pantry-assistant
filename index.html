What can I cook?

Recipes calculate ingredient quantities for your selected servings. v0.6 explains match confidence, units and missing quantities; possible substitutions are suggestions only. Missing quantities can be sent to your shopping list. Items past their date are excluded automatically; a date alone does not establish food safety.

Weekly meal plan

Add meals from your recipe library. The plan reserves quantities for grocery calculations, but does not deduct inventory until you actually cook.

Combined grocery needs

Totals are calculated across all planned meals, then current usable pantry stock and existing shopping quantities are subtracted once. Incompatible measurements remain separate.

Your data

Data stays on this device in this browser's local storage. Local HTML files can have unstable storage on Android. Export a JSON backup after meaningful changes, and before switching devices or browsers.

v0.1–v0.3 backups can be imported. Older backups contain no recipe favorites or custom recipes.
